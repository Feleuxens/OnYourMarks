# Quick start

Here is the shortest path to a start with false-start detection. Once it works, the [feature pages](README.md#features) explain each part and its settings.

## Requirements

- An iPhone with iOS 27 and OnYourMarks installed.
- A tripod or anything that holds the phone still. Handheld works, but it is less precise.
- Enough light. The camera films at 120 frames per second and needs more light than a normal video.

> For speakers, see [Audio output](features/audio-output.md). The iPhone's own speaker is the most accurate choice.

---

## Set up the phone

### Step 1 - Place the camera

Put the phone beside the track, never on it, a few metres from the start line and slightly in front of it. Every athlete must be in the picture from head to foot, also in the set position. Athletes are numbered from the left as the camera sees them.

### Step 2 - Turn on detection

Open the app and tap **Detection off** below the picture. Allow camera access when iOS asks. The button now reads **Detection on** and the picture shows the athletes.

### Step 3 - Choose the start type

Tap **Block** or **Standing** at the top.

---

## Run a start

Tap **Start**. The app plays:

1. "On your marks" after 2 seconds.
2. "Set" 12 seconds later (block starts only).
3. The gun, 1 to 2 seconds after every athlete holds still. The exact hold is random, so nobody can guess it.

If an athlete moves before the gun, the app fires the recall shots and shows who went early. Otherwise it shows each athlete's reaction time, for example `150 ms ± 7 ms`.

> [!NOTE]
> The gun waits until every athlete in the picture holds still. An athlete who keeps fidgeting delays it; after 3 attempts or 10 seconds the start is cancelled.

---

## Look at the start again

Tap **Review start** under the picture. You can step through the start frame by frame, with the gun and each athlete's first movement marked on the timeline. The next start replaces this recording unless you tap **Save**.
